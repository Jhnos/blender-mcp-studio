## Mechanical acceptance boundary

V5 is a fit prototype, not a strength-qualified part. Printed pins are separate loose-fit
parts; their heads stop inward travel but there is no axial lock against withdrawal.
Use temporary external retention for a static coupon; do not operate motors or load the
chain until a positive retention design and physical fit have been accepted.

The shallow foot transition is 38.66 degrees to the disc face; the longer tangential
gusset ramp is 34.99 degrees. Vertical ear sidewalls remain above the sloped foot.
Changing to metal pins/bearings requires a separate tolerance and bearing-seat revision.
The V5 prototype intentionally has plain Ø4.5 mm bores, not V4's MR84 counterbores.

The generic motion check samples adjacent body surfaces at 15 angles in ±34 degrees;
it does not certify continuous motion, cable bending, pin retention, fatigue or loading.
