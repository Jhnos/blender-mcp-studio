# Reinforced in-disc hollow hinge — V5

**Status:** SUPERSEDED by V6

## Goal and context

One repeatable body with orthogonal front/rear side hinges, open centre, shallow reinforced
roots and printable pins. [V4 history](tendon-hinge-v4.md) preceded this dimensional contract.

## Accepted V5 contract

- Disc Ø34 × 4 mm; complete body 34 × 34 × 29.6 mm; 20 mm pitch.
- Centre Ø10 mm; four Ø2.4 mm tendon holes at radius 13 mm.
- Foot flares in both directions; printed Ø4 mm shaft with one Ø6 mm head.
- Nine bodies and sixteen pins passed sampled clearance, motion, STL and MCP checks.
- Body warnings remained for slicer review; there was no positive pin withdrawal retention.

and generated outputs remain under ignored `tmp/inset-hinge-v5/`.
